Checkout

Four ways to make checkout feel more delightful, each on top of the real checkout (choosing who's playing, the checks and the test payment all work). Open a court booking or a program month. Every option works on phones and in Arabic.

  1. Now
    Current

    Booking for, terms and Pay on the left; the booking summary on the right.

  2. A
    Ticket in the making

    The summary card becomes the ticket: who's playing fills in live as you choose, a tear line, and a blurred QR that "unlocks" when you pay.

  3. B
    Reassurance

    Progress across the whole booking (with the done steps ticked), what's included at the domes, and what happens after you pay.

  4. C
    Motion

    Small rewarding moments: ticks draw in, cards lift, the total counts to its new value, a missed step nudges, and Pay fills while payment opens.

  5. D
    Friendly + pay bar

    Warmer words ("Almost there", "Who's playing?") and, on phones, a bar with the total and Pay that never scrolls away.
