FAQ

How long is a booking?

Courts are booked in 1.5-hour slots. Pick as many slots and days as you like, up to 3 months ahead.

Do I need an account to book?

Yes. Sign in or create an account when you continue to booking. It only takes a minute, and your details fill in at checkout.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. At checkout, choose "Someone else" under Booking for and add the player's name and mobile number.

What should I bring?

Sportswear and the right shoes for the court. The clubhouse has washrooms and showers for men and women.

When should I arrive?

Check in at the front desk a few minutes before your slot. You'll be on the court in minutes.

What are the court hours?

Sunday to Wednesday 9 AM to 12 AM, Thursday 9 AM to 1 AM, Friday 8 AM to 1 AM, and Saturday 8 AM to 12 AM.

Is there parking?

Yes, SFA Domes Dammam has 250 parking spots.

Can I pay by cash?

No. All payments must be made upon booking through the SFA App, with the provision of multiple online payment options.

What are the dimensions of the fields/courts?

The dimensions of all courts are set to professional standards. By selecting a court type, the accompanying description can assist you in determining the number of players allowed.

Do I have to bring my own balls/equipment? Is there equipment that can be rented?

Free balls and bibs are provided for each booking.

Which currency will I pay in?

All prices are in Saudi riyals (SAR), and that is what you pay.
