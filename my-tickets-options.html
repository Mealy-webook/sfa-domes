Booking page

Round 2: three directions built from a benchmark of 21 booking pages (GetYourGuide, Booking.com, Agoda, Kayak, Turo, Navan, Eventbrite, Klook, DICE, ClassPass and others). All three follow the same ten rules: the status as a sentence with the countdown, one main action, manage actions in one place with the cancellation deadline right above Cancel, every person as a row with their own status, the ref in the header, the payment as a receipt, and help at the end. Try each on an open game for you and 2 guests, a program for 2 children and a court booked for 3 times.

  1. 1
    Manage rail

    Like GetYourGuide, Navan, Kayak and Turo on web. Content on the left: status sentence, players and tickets (or program cards), booking details, receipt. A sticky Manage booking card on the right: the main action and Get directions, send guest tickets, view confirmation, then the free-cancellation deadline over Cancel; Need help under it. On phones the card comes right after the tickets.

    Like Turo, Agoda and Kayak in their apps. A summary stays on top (photo, status sentence, ref, where, how many tickets, the main action), then tabs that each answer one question: Tickets, Details (with Manage booking and the deadline), Receipt, Help (contact and good to know). The tab bar sticks under the header.

  3. 3
    Order and attendees

    Like Eventbrite, Live Nation and Klook. "Booking SFA-…" as the title with its actions as links beside it, the status sentence, an order card (what, when, where | booked by, booked on, total, payment, tickets, free cancellation), then a table with one row per person (or time): ticket number, delivery, balance, status and actions. Tick guests to send several tickets at once; a program card opens inside its row. Receipt and help at the end.

My bookings

Four ways to restructure the My tickets tab of My account. Each organises the whole page differently; open a booking from any of them to see its tickets, send a guest their ticket or remove one person. They run on the demo account: an open game for you and 2 guests, a kids' game for 2 children, a court booked for 3 times, and several program months.

  1. 1
    By booking type

    Tabs for Courts, Open games, Programs (and Ladies hours when there are any), each laid out for what it is: courts as reservations with their times as buttons, open games as match cards with the players, programs as subscriptions with a session strip, progress and renewal. Past and cancelled fold away underneath.

    Open
  2. 2
    Family

    Organised by person: everyone's next session at a glance, then pick a person to see their next ticket with its QR and everything they're booked into. Guests get their own view with each guest's ticket and Send.

    Open
  3. 3
    Calendar

    A month calendar with a coloured dot for every session (courts, games, programs, ladies hours). Pick a day to see its sessions, who's playing and each ticket; the next sessions are listed beside it.

    Open
  4. 4
    Today hub

    Today's tickets first, ready to scan for everyone; then To do (send guest tickets, renewals, free cancellation ending soon), active programs with progress, and what's coming up by Tomorrow / This week / Later. A booking opens in a side panel.

    Open
