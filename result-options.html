Confirmation page

Fifteen whole-page directions for the booking confirmation, next to the live one. Every option works with real bookings: open a court booked for Yousef (3 sessions over 2 days) or a program month (12 sessions). Each page is responsive and switches to Arabic like the live one.

Redesign from the benchmark · 10 Nov

Three new directions built on the confirmation benchmark. None shows QR codes or directions on the page (they live in My tickets), each says the QR works on the session day, and each offers the next booking. No cancellation rule yet.
